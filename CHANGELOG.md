# Changelog

Each `## <version>` section is that version's release notes: the release
workflow publishes it as the GitHub Release text, which the Ragnarok Offline
app shows players under "What's new". Write it for players.

## 0.1.1
- The Costume Himmelmez's Wig Selection Box now opens the game's selection
  window, like the Booster costume boxes: pick a wig colour and confirm.
  Needs app 1.7.2 or later.

## 0.1.0
- First release: kRO's 24th anniversary event. Visit the 24th Anniversary
  Coin Exchanger in Prontera (156, 282) once a day for your Attendance Box,
  20 days in all, each with a gift and 24th Anniversary Coins. Every account
  also gets one 24th Anniversary Gift Box with three All in One Potions and
  a 7-day Kafra Buff.
- Spend your coins at the Exchanger: World Tour Ticket Envelopes, Small Mana
  Potions, life potion and defense scroll boxes, the Infinity All Booster Box,
  Advanced Battle Manuals, Bubble Gum, Tokens of Siegfried, Costume Tris
  Sword, Costume Tan Spear, and a Costume Himmelmez's Wig in the colour of
  your choice.
- Prefer kRO's way? Turn on "Use the Attendance Check window" in the mod's
  settings and the boxes arrive by mail through the game's Attendance Check
  window instead. Don't use it together with standart-npc's Attendance
  Check: only one attendance can run at a time.
- Known issue: World Tour Tickets don't take you anywhere yet. This server
  doesn't support travelling with them.
