# anniversary-24th

kRO's 24th anniversary items (2026) for Ragnarok Offline. Renewal only. Sources: the kRO client (build of 2026-10-21: `System/itemInfo_true.lub`), gnjoy's event page (attendance table and the Coin Exchanger's list) and divine-pride.net (AegisNames and English names). English text for items the boxes hold is ROenglishRE's; the rest is translated for this mod.

## Setting

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `client_attendance` | off | The 20 attendance boxes come from the game's Attendance Check window, one a calendar day by mail, as on kRO. The Exchanger then only gives the gift box. Off: the Exchanger hands the boxes out. Only one attendance can run at a time, so don't switch it on together with standart-npc's Attendance Check: the server keeps one and skips the other. |

## Deviations from kRO

- kRO ran the event from 2026-08-05 to 2026-09-01 and deletes the items afterwards. This mod has no end date and keeps the items.
- kRO gave the attendance boxes through its login attendance window and the gift box to every account that logged in. Here the Coin Exchanger hands them out by default: one attendance box a day per account, Day 1 to Day 20 in order, and one gift box per account. The setting `client_attendance` uses the Attendance Check window for the boxes instead.
- The attendance window's period runs from kRO's start date (2026-08-05) to 2099, since kRO's end date has passed.
- `System/CheckAttendance.lub` is always loaded, since the app can't switch client files by setting. With the setting off it only changes what the window lists. If standart-npc is also installed, its list wins, because it loads after this mod.
- kRO's Exchanger offers one pick per price row; the barter shop simply lists every item with its price.
- The All in One Potion no longer gives +20 to all stats, which stock rAthena added and neither client lists. Its other strengths are stock rAthena's.
- The coin and gift box use icons iRO's data.grf has (the 20th anniversary gold coin and a gift box): kRO's own icons for them are only in kRO's data.grf. The mod ships no art.
- Known issue: rAthena has no handler for the World Tour Ticket's Navigation travel, so the tickets from the envelope do nothing.

Values with one source are marked `[unverified]` where they're used.

## What to check in game

1. Talk to the Coin Exchanger at prontera 156,282 and take today's box. Open it: you get the gift and the coins. Talking again says to come back tomorrow.
2. Take the gift box, open it, and drink a potion: life potions, defense scroll, Almighty, Infinity Drink, Tyr's Blessing, Power Booster, mana potion and movement speed for an hour.
3. Exchange coins (`@item 1003076 45`): the shop lists 11 trades. Buy the 40-coin box and open it: it asks which wig colour.
4. Hover the new items: English names and text, with icons.
